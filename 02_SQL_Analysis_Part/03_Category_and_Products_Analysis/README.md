📦 Product Analytics | Product Performance, Financial Value & Profitability

A Comprehensive SQL-Based Product Analytics Project

Evaluating Product Performance, Measuring Profitability & Optimizing Product Portfolio Decisions

---

🚀 Project Overview

The Product Analytics Project is an end-to-end analytical initiative designed to evaluate product performance, financial contributions, and profitability across the company's product portfolio.

Using Microsoft SQL Server, this project explores product-level and category-level sales data to uncover revenue patterns, assess product profitability, evaluate financial efficiency, and identify the most valuable products and product categories.

The analysis integrates category profitability evaluation with comprehensive product-level reporting to provide a deeper understanding of product performance and its contribution to overall business profitability.

The ultimate objective is to transform product and transactional data into actionable business intelligence that supports product portfolio optimization, pricing strategies, resource allocation, and data-driven commercial decisions.

---

🎯 Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

- 📦 Evaluate product performance across different categories and subcategories.
- 💰 Measure revenue, cost, profit, and profit margins at the product level.
- 📊 Identify the most profitable product categories.
- 🏆 Rank products according to their financial contributions and profitability.
- 📈 Evaluate product sales frequency, volume, and purchasing behavior.
- ⏱️ Analyze product sales recency and historical sales activity.
- 🎯 Identify high-margin products and financially valuable product groups.
- 💡 Support strategic product management and profitability optimization decisions.

---

📂 Project Structure

The Product Analytics section consists of two specialized analytical reports, each addressing a different dimension of product performance and profitability.

01. 💰 Category Profitability Analysis

File: "01_Category_Profitability_Analysis.sql"

Objective:

Analyze financial performance and profitability across different product categories to understand how each category contributes to the company's overall revenue and profit.

Key Analytical Areas:

- Revenue generated by each product category.
- Revenue contribution percentage.
- Total quantities sold across categories.
- Total product costs.
- Absolute profit by category.
- Profit margin evaluation.
- Comparative category profitability analysis.

Business Value:

Enables management to identify the most profitable product categories, evaluate the relationship between revenue generation and operating costs, and make more informed decisions regarding marketing investments, resource allocation, and product portfolio strategies.

---

02. 📊 End-to-End Product Analytics Report

File: "02_Products_Report.sql"

Objective:

Develop a comprehensive product-level analytical report that integrates product attributes, sales behavior, financial performance, and profitability into a unified product reporting view.

Key Analytical Areas:

Product Profile:

- Product identification and descriptive attributes.
- Product lines and categories.
- Subcategory classification.
- Product cost and start date.

Sales Behavior:

- Total orders.
- Total quantities sold.
- Average quantity per order.
- Average unit selling value.
- Average order value.
- Monthly sales frequency.

Product Engagement:

- Sales recency.
- Historical sales activity.
- Product age.
- Sales lifespan.

Financial Performance:

- Total revenue.
- Total cost.
- Total profit.
- Product profit margins.

Product Ranking & Segmentation:

- Ranking by total profit contribution.
- Ranking by profit margin.
- Profitability-based product classification.

Business Value:

Provides a unified product-level reporting foundation that enables management to evaluate individual product performance, identify high-value products, understand sales behavior, and prioritize products according to their financial contributions and profitability.
